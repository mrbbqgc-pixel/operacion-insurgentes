Ciudad Insurgentes · Comondú · Baja California Sur

Operación Insurgentes

A mercenary force has seized the town and the crossroads where Highway 1 turns east for Loreto. Lead fireteam Halcón up Blvd. Benito Juárez, take the Glorieta, and clear the Plaza Principal.

One player creates a game and sends the invite link or the room code to the others. Up to 8 players, phones and computers together. Your devices connect to each other directly, so play with people you know.

Room code
-----

    Keyboard and mouse

    WASD move, Shift sprint, C crouch, Space jump. Left click fire, right click aim. R reload, Q swap, G grenade, V knife, F pick up or use, M map, Esc pause.

    Phone or tablet

    Left thumb moves, right thumb aims. Push the stick all the way forward to sprint. FIRE can be dragged to keep aiming, and it fires by itself when your sights are on a hostile. Turn the phone sideways.

    The street grid, street names and landmarks follow the real town at about 60% scale: Blvd. Benito Juárez, the Glorieta, the Plaza Principal, the parish, schools, clinics, churches and the Panteón Municipal sit on their real blocks (place data: Google Maps). Houses and shops are generated. The enemy force is fictional.
